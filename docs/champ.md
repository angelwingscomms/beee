Dear Parent, School Administrator and Prospective Participant,

The BEEE® Spectacular Chess Championship is a transformative youth development experience that combines competitive chess with AI-powered learning, leadership development, mentorship, and personal growth. 

Designed for chess players aged 10 to 14 years in schools across Abuja, the championship combines e4™, TEAMUP™  (Technology, Enterprise, Art, Mentorship and Upskill) and TASKIFY™ self Development Programmes to deliver a structured journey of learning, achievementq and competition that extends far beyond the chessboard.

The Championship Journey 

August 1 to September 19, 2026

Commencing August 1, 2026 with online engagement, participants complete activities, assessments, and challenges in advanced chess training, mentored learning and project-based, personal development evaluations - while advancing to the preliminary live chess series and an immersive championship experience scheduled for October, 2026. 

A comprehensive development pathway

Stage 1: Registration and Enrollment
Participants register through participating schools and gain access to the e4 , TASKIFY and TEAMUP™  (Technology, Enterprise, Art, Mentorship Upskill) and apps and modules.

Stage 2: Innovative Learning and Development Tools
At the heart of the BEEE Chess Championship is the e4™, TEAMUP™ and TASKIFY™ Development Programmes. These innovative learning tools guide participants through a pathway that nurtures and support whole child development.

September 2026 to October 2026

Stage 3: Preliminary Championship Rounds
Participants compete in exciting live preliminary events and the qualifying stages.  

Stage 4: Elite Qualification Stage 
Qualifying participants continue their developmental journey while advancing through higher levels of championship competition and developing greater competence in:
- Collaboration
- Coordination
- Strategic communication¹
- Team Work

Qualifying participants compete for finalists and runner-up positions.

Stage 5: Championship Grand Finale
Finalists will participate in an immersive championship experience, a unique championship finale unlike conventional chess competitions. 
Details of the finale format will be revealed in Stage 4 of the championship journey.

About e4™

The e4™ is a chess training app where players can compete against a virtual opponent while being mentored by an AI coach. 

Being able to predict every move, it can suggest the next best move for the player, analyse mistakes, answer questions  and  improve the players level through voice prompts and text.

About TASKIFY™ 

Participants will not simply attend sessions—they will embark on a measurable development journey. Every registered participant will receive access to a personal TASKIFY™ Development Passport. 

Through a digital progress-tracking system, participants will build their own TASKIFY™  Development Passport, earning milestones, badges, achievement points, certificates, valuable skills and recognition awards as they progress through various stages of the programme.

Progress may be assessed through:
- Skill development milestones
- Chess performance assessments
- Innovation and creativity challenges
- Leadership and teamwork activities
- Mentorship participation
- Project completion
- Attendance and consistency records
- Reflection and personal growth exercises
- Championship accomplishments

This system provides participants, parents, and schools with a visible track record of achievement throughout the championship cycle.
