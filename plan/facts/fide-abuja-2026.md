# Fact sheet: FIDE World Amateur Rapid and Blitz Chess Championships, Abuja 2026

This file is the only source for the first news post. Every name, score, date, title, and

## The event

- Full name: FIDE World Amateur Rapid and Blitz Chess Championships 2026.
- Host city: Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria.
- Venue: Bolton White Hotel, Abuja. The playing hall is the Bolton White Event Hall.
- Dates: 1 to 8 August 2026. Players arrived on 1 August and left on 8 August.
- The rapid was played from 2 to 5 August. The blitz was played on 6 and 7 August.
- Organisers: FIDE and the Nigeria Chess Federation.
- This was the first time Africa hosted the FIDE World Amateur Championships.
- Entry was open to players with a FIDE rating under 2300.
- Six titles per event: Open and Women, in three rating bands, under 2300, under 2000,
  and under 1700.
- Each event was 11 rounds of Swiss pairing.
- Rapid time control: 15 minutes plus 10 seconds a move. Write it as `15+10`.
- Blitz time control: 3 minutes plus 2 seconds a move. Write it as `3+2`.
- Women players were eligible for two prizes. Men were eligible for one.

- Ekunke Odey Goodness is Nigerian and was 14 years old. He is the first FIDE World
  Amateur Rapid champion, because 2026 was the first year of the event.
- Kigigha took bronze on tie-break, ahead of Unwana's equal score. Unwana took silver.
- Joseph Sambo is Nigerian and was 17 years old.
